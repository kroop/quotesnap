# QuoteSnap — Money machine

## Why this product (not another invoice app)

| | BillFast (prior) | QuoteSnap (this) |
|--|------------------|------------------|
| Job | Get paid after work | **Win the job before work** |
| Emotion | Admin | **Closing money** |
| Buyer | Anyone who bills | Freelancers / service businesses quoting work |
| Wrong room | Accounting departments | Same — we **don’t** sell to controllers |

Quotes happen **before** cash. People pay more to look legit when a deal is on the line.

## Distribution lessons applied

1. Talk to **owners & freelancers**, not r/AccountingDepartment  
2. Reddit filters hate multi-link promo → soft posts, one link, DMs  
3. Skip paid IH until profitable  
4. Lead with **free tool**; Pro after print (in-app nudge)  
5. Scoreboard = **net $/week**, not upvotes
